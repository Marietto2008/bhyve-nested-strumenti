# How AI was used

- **Code and tests.** Most of the code, the test scripts, the measurements and the reports were produced with
  Claude (Anthropic) working on my machine, under my direction.
- **Reviews.** During development, design reviews were also done by AI models (GPT and Claude models), not by
  people. Nothing here has had independent human review yet. That review is what I am asking for.
- **KVM.** KVM is GPL. It was used as a reference for *behaviour*: it was run as an L0 to compare against, and its
  code was read. It was not copied or translated into bhyve.
- **Commits.** Commits written with Claude carry a `Co-Authored-By: Claude` line.
- **Numbers.** Every number in `RESULTS.md` and in the full report is generated by a script from raw output files,
  all of them in `full-report/data/`, so anyone can check them.
